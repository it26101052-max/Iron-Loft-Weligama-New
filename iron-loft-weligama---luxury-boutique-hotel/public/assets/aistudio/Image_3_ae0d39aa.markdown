## Brand & Style

The design system embodies understated coastal brutalism and architectural luxury. Designed for discerning global travelers, design connoisseurs, and wellness seekers arriving at the southern coast of Sri Lanka, the aesthetic balances the monolithic weight of raw charcoal concrete and black iron with the tactile warmth of tropical alabaster stone, sun-bleached linen, and brushed bronze.

The aesthetic direction is strictly Architectural Minimalism with Editorial High-End Hospitality influences:
- **Atmospheric Restraint:** Lavish whitespace and asymmetric editorial layouts that evoke silent sanctuary and calm.
- **Tonal Contrast:** Deliberate interplay between sunlit stone textures and deep, shadowed charcoal monoliths.
- **Architectural Precision:** Razor-thin dividers, structural framing, and strict geometric proportions reflecting bespoke tropical modernism.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from Weligama’s coastal geological tones: volcanic ironstone, sun-warmed travertine, and antique tropical bronze.

- **Primary (`#121212`):** Pure matte charcoal iron. Serves as primary typography color, high-contrast framing elements, and authoritative solid surfaces in light mode.
- **Secondary (`#C2A676`):** Warm antique bronze. Used for restrained interactive accents, active states, key booking highlights, and subtle ornamental indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#9E7D47`):** Deep burnished bronze. Used for focused states, borders on dark interactive elements, and secondary status markers.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0`):** Alabaster stone. Forms the foundational canvas base, giving interfaces the organic tactile quality of mineral plaster rather than sterile digital white.

### Extended Tonal Applications
- **Surfaces:** Canvas (`#F9F6F0`), Container Base (`#F3EFE6`), Elevated Surface / Cards (`#EAE5D9`), Inverted Monolith Surface (`#1A1A1A`).
- **Dividers & Hairlines:** Low-contrast stone strokes (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.08)`) and muted bronze borders (`rgba(194, 166, 118, 0.35)`).
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text (`#121212`), Secondary text (`#5A564F`), On-Dark / Inverted text (`#F9F6F0`).

## Typography

The typographical pairing relies on the juxtaposition of high-literary classical structure and contemporary geometric utility.

- **EB Garamond** acts as the primary display and editorial voice. Used for large scale numbers, suite titles, hospitality narratives, and arrival moments. Headlines feature deliberate regular weights (`400`) to evoke an authentic literary and gallery demeanor.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers clarity across interface operations, room specifications, rates, booking workflows, and navigation. 
- **Uppercase Tracking:** All metadata labels, categories, and actions (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`) must be rendered in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.12em` to `0.16em`) to echo architectural inscriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a strict, asymmetrical architectural grid with generous structural gutters and intentional negative space.

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid layout contained to a maximum width of `1600px`. Standard margin is `4rem`, with `2rem` gutters. Wide margins maintain spatial breathing room reminiscent of spacious gallery halls.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standard margins compress to `2rem` with `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Standard margins set to `1.25rem` with `1rem` gutters. Editorial hero text wraps with intentional line breaks to maintain vertical balance.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are partitioned by generous vertical gaps using `space-xl` (`5rem`) to `7.5rem` steps, rejecting cramped layouts in favor of unhurried visual cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, floating drop shadows or heavy skeuomorphic extrusions. Instead, depth is established through **architectural stratification, tonal layering, and ultra-crisp hairline intersections**.

- **Surface Tiers:** Layering occurs purely through stone-derived tonal shifts: Base canvas (`#F9F6F0`) → Card/Container (`#F3EFE6`) → Inset/Accentuated Container (`#EAE5D9`).
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Modals, sticky navigation bars, and compartmentalized modules use a single 1px hairline border tinted with `#121212` at 8% opacity (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.08)`) or muted bronze at 20% opacity.
- **Monolithic Inversion:** High-priority overlays (such as booking sheets, suite inspection drawers, and primary bottom bars) invert into `#1A1A1A` charcoal monoliths floating directly over the alabaster ground with zero blur shadow, framed by a 1px border of `rgba(194, 166, 118, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The shape system strictly uses razor-sharp, rectilinear geometries (`roundedness: 0`). 

All corners for cards, buttons, dialogs, inputs, image containers, and floating panels are crisp (`0px` radius). This unyielding geometric discipline directly echoes cast-concrete slabs, blackened iron framing, and linear tropical architectural pavilions, rejecting the generic softness of consumer applications.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Monolith):** Solid matte charcoal (`#121212`) background, crisp `0px` corners, white-alabaster text (`#F9F6F0`), uppercase `label-md` typography. Minimum height `48px`, horizontal padding `2rem`. Hover state introduces a smooth transition to burnished bronze background (`#9E7D47`).
- **Secondary Action (Wireframe):** Transparent background with a 1px solid `#121212` border. Text in `#121212`. Hover inverts to solid `#121212` background with `#F9F6F0` text.
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Pure text button using `label-md`, accompanied by a thin trailing bronze glyph (`→`) and a persistent 1px underline positioned 4px below text.

### Cards (Suites & Experiences)
- Minimalist stone slabs using `#F3EFE6` or bordered with 1px `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.08)`.
- Images are aspect-ratio locked (4:5 or 16:9), flush to top/sides with zero border radius. On hover, imagery responds with a slow, understated zoom (scale 1.03 over 800ms ease-out).
- Content block below the image features generous internal padding (`space-md`), featuring suite names in `headline-sm` EB Garamond, accompanied by room capacity metadata in uppercase `label-sm`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Underlined architectural inputs: transparent background, 1px bottom border in `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.2)`.
- On focus, bottom border transitions to `#C2A676` (muted bronze) with no outline glow.
- Floating placeholder or top-aligned labels set in uppercase `label-sm` with active bronze accent.

### Chips & Tags
- Rectangular blocks with `0px` radius.
- Background in `#EAE5D9`, text in `#121212` (`label-sm`).
- Active selection state switches background to `#121212` and text to `#F9F6F0`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp square boxes (`16px × 16px`) with 1px border in `#121212`.
- Checked state: solid `#121212` fill with a miniature centered bronze accent dot (`#C2A676`) rather than standard checkmarks.

### Booking Bar & Sticky Amenities Navigator
- A full-width anchored strip or floating monolith block featuring inline date selection, occupancy selectors, and booking submission.
- Uses `#1A1A1A` matte charcoal surface with subtle bronze hairline dividers between segments. Text rendered in `#F9F6F0`.
